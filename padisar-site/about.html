About Us

Padisar Inc is an information and communication technology company serving banks and organizations. Since 1996, we have focused on dependable, secure solutions that meet regulatory and operational needs.

Our Story

Established in 1996, our teams have supported core banking integrations, network transformations, data center initiatives, and long-term managed services. We partner with clients for the long run.

  • Customer success through measurable outcomes
